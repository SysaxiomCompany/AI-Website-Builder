About the AI

What is learned, what is rule-based, and how well it works. Every number on this page is read from this project's own measurement files (/api/ml/info, /api/eval and the local ratings); none is typed in by hand and none is taken from the base paper.

Read the numbers with care

The classifiers were trained and tested on prompts written by the project team from templates, not on prompts from real users. The scores show how well the models learn that style of writing. They are not general accuracy, and real prompts will usually score lower. The second table, on a different set of prompts with unseen wording, shows how much lower.

      What is learned and what is not

      Learned (trained on the project's data)

        Rule-based or template assembly

        • Reading the site name, tagline, email, phone, location, color and tone words (regular expressions and word lists)
        • Choosing the color palette and font stack from color and tone words
        • Section order, and the rule that navigation bar, hero and footer are always present
        • Selecting and filling section templates from the team-written content bank (sample text, editable)
        • Rendering the HTML, CSS and JavaScript, escaping all text, building the ZIP

        Base paper and approved changes

        Based on: Reddy, Bodhke, Patil, Gorad, Chavan and Mall, "AI Website Builder", International Journal of Creative Research Thoughts (IJCRT), Vol. 13, Issue 10, October 2025, paper ID IJCRTBH02018. DOI: not provided in the source.

        This project does not reuse the paper's reported accuracy, satisfaction or generation-time figures (the authors measured their own system on an undescribed dataset); it measures its own, shown above.

        Deviations from the paper (Change Control)

        • scikit-learn TF-IDF classifiers instead of transformer models, spaCy and Hugging Face downloads.
        • A template library with rule-based theme and layout selection instead of generative design models and reinforcement learning (there is no reinforcement-learning component).
        • A local Flask app with ZIP export instead of Flask or Django deployed on Vercel.
        • SQLite and files instead of separate template and content databases.
        • The training prompts and the held-out evaluation prompts were authored by the team as templated, seeded text (no real-user data existed), so measured accuracy is optimistic.
        • Models were trained with the team's generic ML-Training-App text pipeline, extended with an optional multi-label function; the app itself loads only plain scikit-learn and joblib files from ml/artifacts/.
